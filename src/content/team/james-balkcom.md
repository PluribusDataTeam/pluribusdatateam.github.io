Mr. Balkcom brings extensive experience across military service, entrepreneurship, and business leadership to his advisory role at Pluribus Inc. His career highlights include serving as Chairman of EndoChoice since 2009 and Lead Director of Reach Health. He founded and operates Council Capital, L.P., and has held executive positions at numerous organizations including eVault, Inc., and Techsonic Industries (Humminbird®), which he led as owner and CEO from 1976 to 1995.

His military background includes graduating from West Point in 1967, with decorations including a Bronze Star with Oak Leaf Cluster and the Vietnamese Distinguished Service Medal. He holds an MBA in Finance from Harvard Business School and has served as Civilian Aide to the Secretary of the Army for Georgia since 2002.

Mr. Balkcom resides in Atlanta with Linda, his wife of 45 years, and has two daughters and five grandchildren.
