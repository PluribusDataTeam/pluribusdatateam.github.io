Ambassador Gentry O. Smith serves as a Strategic Advisor to Pluribus Inc., recognized as a global leader in diplomatic security and national defense. He brings over 30 years of distinguished U.S. Department of State service, with expertise spanning more than 170 countries including Cairo, Tokyo, and Yangon. Most recently, he was the 11th Assistant Secretary for Diplomatic Security, overseeing a $6 billion budget and leading 48,000 personnel globally.

Ambassador Smith has advanced public safety through roles in the Office of Foreign Missions, Diplomatic Security, and Security Countermeasures. He has collaborated with Fortune 500 Chief Security Officers, major corporations, faith-based organizations, and NGOs to strengthen security resilience in high-risk areas.

As a trusted government advisor, he has worked alongside over 400 ambassadors, the U.S. Intelligence Community, military leadership, and the National Security Council. He has protected three U.S. Secretaries of State and contributed expertise to major global events including the World Cup and the Olympics for counterterrorism and cybersecurity measures.

He holds a Bachelor of Arts in Political Science from North Carolina State University and possesses Top Secret/Sensitive Compartmented Information (TS/SCI) security clearances, along with certifications in security investigations and high-threat foreign deployments.
