Mr. Samuel established Pluribus Inc., a geospatial analytics firm based in McLean, Virginia, focusing on Identity-Based Navigation®. His career includes serving as a Tier-2 Defense Intelligence Senior Executive with over 30 years of federal service. His positions encompassed roles such as National Geospatial Intelligence Officer, NGA Operations Center Director, and country lead for Djibouti. He also served as a U.S. Air Force pilot with more than 1,000 flight hours and as an F-15C fighter pilot.

Before founding Pluribus, he held the position of Chief of Congressional Affairs at the National Geospatial-Intelligence Agency, managing personnel through multiple congressional hearings and budget cycles. His efforts secured a $1.7 billion appropriation for NGA's St. Louis campus expansion, earning the 2017 Intelligence Community Legislative Affairs Team of the Year award.

His educational credentials include a B.S. in General Engineering from the U.S. Air Force Academy, an M.A. in Information Technology from Webster University, and executive education at Harvard's Kennedy School. He holds multiple patents and has received the Presidential Rank Award and the BAAE Entrepreneur of the Year Award. He is married to Dr. Evelyn Teague Samuel.
