Daniel Neustadter is a Development Operations, Data and Cyber Engineer at Pluribus Inc., joining the company after serving as a Software Development Operations Airman with the U.S. Air Force's BESPIN Software Factory at Maxwell Air Force Base, Alabama.

At BESPIN, Daniel worked to support Air Force applications on AWS GovCloud and Cloud One, helped build out the organization's penetration testing program, and led threat-hunting efforts that remediated hundreds of vulnerabilities. He also helped build and harden a secure enterprise network supporting more than 100 personnel.

He holds a security clearance and the following credentials:

- B.S., Cybersecurity & Information Assurance, Western Governors University
- A.S., Computer Science Technology, Community College of the Air Force
