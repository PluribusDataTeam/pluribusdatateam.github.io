Daniel Neustadter serves as a Development Operations, Data and Cyber Engineer at Pluribus Inc., having previously worked as a Software Development Operations Airman with the U.S. Air Force's BESPIN Software Factory at Maxwell Air Force Base, Alabama. During his Air Force tenure, he supported applications on AWS GovCloud and Cloud One, contributed to building the organization's penetration testing program, and spearheaded threat-hunting initiatives that resolved hundreds of vulnerabilities. He also assisted in constructing and securing an enterprise network serving over 100 personnel.

He maintains a security clearance and holds multiple advanced degrees, including an M.S. and B.S. in Cybersecurity & Information Assurance from Western Governors University, plus an A.S. in Computer Science Technology from the Community College of the Air Force.

His certifications span cloud, security, and infrastructure domains, including credentials from ISC2, PMI, AWS, HashiCorp, CompTIA, and LPI. He earned Eagle Scout recognition and volunteers as a Boy Scouts of America instructor throughout the Southeastern United States while contributing to open source projects.
