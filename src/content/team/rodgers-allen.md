Mr. Rodgers Allen, Jr. is the Vice President for Pluribus Inc., a big data, geosocial & geospatial analytics company headquartered in McLean, Virginia. Mr. Allen joined Pluribus to help lead the world in an entirely new industry: Identity-Based Navigation®.

After graduating from the Academy of Mathematics and Science High School in St. Louis, Missouri, Mr. Allen joined the United States Army as a logistician stationed at Fort Bragg, North Carolina, and deployed to Desert Shield/Desert Storm providing logistics support to the 82nd Airborne and 7th Corps Infantry Divisions. He subsequently joined the St. Louis Metropolitan Police Department, leading several investigation bureaus including Internal Affairs and Criminal Investigations, before being recruited by the Federal Bureau of Investigation, where he served on the Joint Terrorism Task Force for eight years.

Mr. Allen was later recruited by the National Geospatial-Intelligence Agency as an imagery analyst, spending 10 years advancing his geospatial expertise before becoming Deputy Director of NGA's Office of Science and Technology, overseeing scientific disciplines, data centers, and cloud portfolios.

Previously, he served as an Executive Account Manager at Amazon Web Services, earning AWS Cloud Practitioner and AI certifications while leading a team generating $500 million in annual revenue for three consecutive years across U.S. Intelligence Community agencies.

A Webster University graduate with a B.A. in Political Science, Mr. Allen has received the National Security Initiative, the Department of Justice Distinguished Service Award, and the Army Achievement Medal. He is married to Marjorie Grace Allen.
