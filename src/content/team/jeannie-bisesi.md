Jeanne Wilkins Bisesi serves as Senior Project Manager at Pluribus Inc., overseeing the Guardian ANJEL® and ANJEL Klaxon platforms. Her background encompasses human factors engineering, neuropsychology, crisis action response, and command and control through a 31-year military career spanning Air Force Active Duty, Reserve, and Air National Guard service in critical leadership positions including plans officer, command post chief, logistics readiness officer, and mission support group commander.

Her notable crisis experience includes serving as crisis action team executive officer during Y2K operations at Yokota Air Force Base and as Command Post Chief during the 9/11 response, coordinating with the Pennsylvania Emergency Management Agency. She participated in the AMC Crisis Battle Staff during COVID-19 operations, the Afghan withdrawal, and multiple hurricane responses (Michael, Florence, Dorian, Sally, Ida, and Helene).

Her civilian background includes published research in functional magnetic resonance imaging, reading assessments, and neurochemical effects on emotional expression. She has worked with behaviorally disabled children at Kennedy Krieger Institute and as a youth counselor.

Her degrees include PhD coursework in Neuroscience at Bowling Green State University, an M.S. in Developmental Psychology from Johns Hopkins University, and a B.S. in Experimental Psychology/Human Factors Engineering from the U.S. Air Force Academy.

She lists her greatest achievement as being mother to five beautiful children: Jim, Leo, Isabel, Lily, and Joe.
